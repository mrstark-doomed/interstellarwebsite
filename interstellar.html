About the Movie

Interstellar (2014) is a science fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan. It explores space, time, and the limits of human courage. The movie’s visuals, emotions, and soundtrack by Hans Zimmer make it truly unforgettable.

Main Cast

  • Matthew McConaughey as Joseph Cooper
  • Anne Hathaway as Dr. Amelia Brand
  • Jessica Chastain as Murphy Cooper
  • Michael Caine as Professor Brand
  • Matt Damon as Dr. Mann
